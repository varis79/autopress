# 01 · BEFORE YOU START — is it for you?

> Honesty first. Autopress is great for **one** kind of media outlet and useless for
> others. Read this before you invest an afternoon.

---

## What it IS for: news-curation outlets

Autopress builds a **media outlet that curates real news on a topic with a live flow of
sources** and publishes a **periodic edition** (usually weekly). It fits when your
topic has a **steady stream of developments with citable sources** (RSS feeds,
newsletters, industry press):

- ✅ Fleets / logistics in Mexico and Spain
- ✅ Cybersecurity, energy, agriculture, fintech, mobility…
- ✅ **Current-affairs** geopolitics (with the `strict` profile, since it's sensitive)
- ✅ Any B2B or industry niche with its own press

Its great strength is **credibility**: it cites every fact with its source (`[1][2]`),
doesn't invent figures, and the core is deterministic and cheap.

## What it is NOT for (important)

- ❌ **Static or historical topics** with no news flow — "18th-century urban planning",
  "geography of such-and-such region". There are no developments to curate and freshness
  doesn't apply.
- ❌ **A magazine of original essays** where the AI *generates* instead of *summarizing
  real facts*. That's a different product (more expensive and with more risk of
  fabrication); the kit is deliberately designed for the opposite.
- ❌ **A simple personal newsletter:** if you just want to write yourself and send, **use
  Ghost, Substack or Beehiiv** — easier, nothing to set up.

> Quick rule: **does your topic have new news every week with sources to link to?**
> If yes → Autopress. If no → it's not the right tool.

---

## What you need (and what you don't)

**You need:**
- A **coding agent** (Claude Code, Cursor…) with terminal and file access. It does the
  technical work.
- A few hours to get started and, if you publish, to create some **free accounts**
  (GitHub, host, Resend).
- **Editorial judgment:** sources, editorial line, and review when the topic is
  sensitive.

**You do NOT need:**
- To know how to program.
- To pay much: **~0.30–1.20 USD/month** + optional domain (~10–15 USD/year). See
  [03-COSTES.md](03-COSTES.md).

---

## What is autonomous and what isn't (realistic expectation)

- **The weekly _edition_ is autonomous:** it ingests, curates, writes and publishes on
  its own.
- **The _judgment_ is NOT autonomous:** you set the rules, the sources and the editorial
  line. For sensitive topics, you review before publishing (`strict` profile).
- It's not "set a topic and make money": the revenue expectation is **close to zero** at
  the start. Build it because you care about the topic.
