# 10 · SEO — done right, without flooding Google

> Technical SEO done well **always**; mass generation of pages with AI **off by
> default**. Google penalizes low-value content at scale; that's not where we're going.

## What the kit already does for you

- **Structured data** JSON-LD (`NewsArticle`) on every edition.
- **Open Graph** for sharing on social/messaging.
- **Sitemap** with `<lastmod>` and **RSS** with the archive history.
- **Canonical** per page and **internal links** (home ↔ archive ↔ editions).
- **`noindex` by default**: only the real production publication is indexed (previews
  and drafts never are).

## The key decision: NO mass generation

- Generating "hundreds of pillar pages" with AI is **OFF by default** on purpose.
- Google treats content at scale that adds little as spam. A curation outlet with
  judgment, citing sources, is the opposite: **real value**.
- If you ever turn on more content, let it be because it adds value, not for volume.

## Best practices (you/your agent)

- **Honest freshness**: update the date only if the content really changed (no
  "fake freshness" rotating dates without changes).
- **Titles and descriptions** clear and specific; no clickbait.
- **Performance**: the site is static and lightweight (no heavy trackers) — that already helps.
- **Custom domain** when you can: it improves authority and brand.

## What NOT to do

- Don't generate pages at scale to "rank keywords".
- Don't duplicate summaries that are nearly identical to the source (paraphrase and add context).
- Don't buy links or use tricks: a penalty is guaranteed in the medium term.
