## How we handle sources

- **We paraphrase facts**, we don't copy the original text. We rewrite it in our own words.
- **We always cite and link** the original source of every claim (little numbers `[1][2]`).
- We use **short extracts** (headline + a brief summary), only as much as needed to inform.
- We respect **each source's terms**: if a feed prohibits reuse or has no clear
  conditions, **we exclude it**.
