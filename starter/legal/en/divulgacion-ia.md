## The content is written by an AI from real sources

<MEDIA_NAME> is a **news curation** outlet. Each edition is produced as follows:

1. An automated system **collects** news from public sources (RSS feeds) about
   <TOPIC>.
2. A deterministic program **classifies, deduplicates and selects** them (no AI).
3. A **language model (AI) writes** the summary of each story **solely from those
   facts**, and **cites and links to each original source**.

We don't invent figures or data: if something isn't in the source, we don't claim it. The
figures in our copy are **automatically verified** against the source text.

## Human oversight

The level of review depends on the topic (risk profile):

- **Sensitive topics** (politics, health, named individuals): **mandatory human review**
  before publishing and **corroboration with ≥2 independent sources**.
- **Everything else**: the edition may be published automatically, but **<EDITOR_IN_CHARGE>**
  retains editorial responsibility and corrects it when warranted.
